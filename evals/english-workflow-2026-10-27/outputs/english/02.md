**Paper Summary**

The note studies range bounds for weighted aggregation of values in [0,1]. It claims that arbitrary nonnegative raw weights preserve this interval. It separately proves the bound for normalized weights and defines a zero output when all weights vanish.

**Strengths**

1. Proposition 2 correctly uses weights summing to one to establish the normalized variant's [0,1] bound. The zero-denominator case is explicitly handled.

**Weaknesses**

1. Theorem 1 is false: take n = 2, w₁ = w₂ = 1, and x₁ = x₂ = 1; then S = 2. Nonnegativity does not imply that the weights sum to at most one, so the proof's crucial inference fails. Proposition 2 concerns a different variant and cannot repair the retained raw-weight claim. Impose a total-weight bound of at most one, or restrict the guarantee to normalized aggregation and revise the advertised scope.

**Justification of Recommendation**

I recommend rejection in its current form: the sole formal guarantee for the raw variant fails under its stated assumptions. The valid normalized result does not support the guarantee for both variants.
